# antislop audit 001, 2026-11-06

Page: niceshirt.rizzo.cc, index.html v0.8.7. Mode: during (global preference).
Design Read: show page for a closed Second City revue, audience = people who saw it or
are looking the cast up, show-poster visual language (Luckiest Guy display, blue on near-black,
gold kicker). Dials: ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.

## HIGH

1. R-03 tap targets. Measured at 375px: nav links 30px tall, hero slide dots 9px, "Read it on"
   links 22px, the inline Cole Steeves bio button 24px, the footer rizzo.cc link 16px. All under 44px.
2. R-27 / C-4 stale state. The run closed 2026-09-21. All eight dates render crossed out, but the hero
   still leads with a primary "Get Tickets" button, the nav CTA still says "Tickets", the tickets lead
   still says "Each performance has its own ticket link", and the FAQ lead says "this September".
   The page has a past state for individual dates but none for the show as a whole.
3. R-02 em dashes. og:title, twitter:title, the four JSON-LD name fields and the llms.txt H1 all use
   "NICE SHIRT — The Second City Conservatory Show". Body copy is clean.
4. R-32 focus. No focus style is removed (good), but none is written either, so keyboard focus relies
   on the browser default ring, which varies and is faint on the dark cards.

## MEDIUM (technique is fine, the reason was never written down)

5. R-06 uppercase with wide tracking on eyebrows, kicker, meta line, tags and roles.
   Reason: playbill and marquee typesetting; it is the show-poster voice the whole page is built on.
6. R-11 pills on buttons, tags, bio links, version and visit counters.
   Reason: pill = action or label, rounded rectangle = container. Radius scale is 16 / 12 / 10 / 8 / pill.
7. R-01 / R-13 glow: title text-shadow, hero radial, next-up ring. Three uses, under the dose cap.
   Reason: marquee bulb glow on the title only; the radial is the stage wash behind it.
8. R-19 hero slideshow auto-advances forever. Reason: the owner asked for the clips to play; MOTION 2;
   it pauses on hidden tabs and the fade shortens under reduce-motion (owner decision, v0.7.3).
9. R-08 arrows on the two "Read it on" links. Reason: outbound-link cue, only on the two external articles.

## LOW

10. R-16 copy. FAQ lead reads as search-engine bait ("anyone finding NICE SHIRT while searching for
    Second City, Chicago improv or sketch comedy shows this September"). Tickets lead describes the
    mechanism ("crossed off automatically") instead of the run.
11. R-25 contrast: every pairing checked with contrast-check.py, lowest is dim text over the brightest
    hero slide at 4.68:1. PASS, nothing to do.

## Decisions

Fixing 1, 2, 3, 4 and 10 in v0.8.8, and writing the reasons for 5 to 9 into the stylesheet as comments.
Item 2 is handled as an honest "the run is over" state, not a redesign: the dates, prices and venue
facts stay as the record of the run.
